How to Play

🌱 Planting

Pick a seed from the shelf, then tap any empty plot in your field to plant it.

✨ Mutations

Every plant rolls a mutation the moment you plant it — watch for a glowing plot and an icon badge. Multiple tiers can stack together for huge combined value.

☀️ Weather

Weather cycles automatically and affects grow speed, sell prices, and mutation odds — check the badge next to your wallet. Watch for the rare 🌅 Golden Hour!

🎒 Inventory

Harvesting doesn't sell instantly — crops go to your Inventory first. Sell them one at a time or all at once whenever you like.

🛠 Skill Tree

Spend coins on upgrades: faster growth, better prices, extra plots, a scarecrow, beehives, and even an auto-harvester. Unlock the root node first.

🧴 Gear Shop

Buy consumables like fertilizers or mutation charms, tap one to arm it, then tap a specific growing plot to apply it there.

📜 Quests & Daily Gift

3 quests are always active. Complete one for XP and coins, and a fresh quest rolls in immediately. Claim your 🎁 Daily Gift at the top of the Quest Board — keep a streak going for bigger rewards.

🏅 Achievements

Lifetime milestones that survive rebirth. Each one pays out coins or even rebirth points the moment you earn it.

⭐ Leveling

Planting, harvesting, and buying upgrades all earn XP. Higher levels unlock rarer, more valuable crops.

♻️ Rebirth

Once you reach level 24, you can rebirth: it resets your run, but pays out permanent rebirth points — 1 per million coins you're holding — to spend on a permanent upgrade shop.

🍳 Kitchen

Cook crops into dishes. Sell them for much more than the raw crops, or eat one for a timed buff (faster growth, higher prices, more XP, better mutation odds). Late-game recipes need crops you may not be able to grow yet — get them from other players.

📋 Buy Orders

In the Trading Post's Orders tab, post a request: "I want 20 tomatoes at 80 🪙 each". Your coins are reserved, any player can deliver some or all of it and gets paid right away, and the items show up in your inventory. Cancel to get unspent coins back. Filling other people's orders is a great way to turn spare crops into coins.

☁️ Account & Saves

Your farm autosaves in this browser. Sign in with Google (Account tab) to back it up to the cloud and play on any device.

🏆 Leaderboard

Signed-in farmers are ranked by lifetime earnings, rebirths, and overall farm rank. Change your display name in the Account tab.

🤝 Trading

List crops from your inventory on the Trading Post for any price. Other players can buy them; you collect the coins (minus a 5% market fee). Leave the item in escrow until it sells, or cancel to take it back. Add a friend's trade code to make a private listing.
